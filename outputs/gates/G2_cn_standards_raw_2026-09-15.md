**Reading rules used in this file**

- `DIRECT_STANDARD_TEXT_SEEN` = the standard's own clause wording was retrieved and read in
  this session (either the standard PDF/preview itself, or a host that reproduces the
  standard's numbered clause text verbatim). The host is named in the row.
- `SECONDARY_ONLY` = only a third party *describes* what the standard says (government
  guidance document, training site, company page). The standard text itself was not read.
- `NOT_OBTAINED` = not verified at all.
- Nothing below is inferred. Every number is quoted from a page listed in §1 or §3.
- No paywalled standard was downloaded or redistributed. Files saved are free/public copies
  (government sites, free standards mirrors, public try-read previews). Only short factual
  numeric extracts with locators are reproduced.

## 5. Semantics warning for the modelling layer (facts only, no recommendation)

- The Chinese tower-crane stop rules are printed as **风速** without an averaging window in the
  operative clauses (JGJ 33-2012 §4.1.14/§4.1.15, JGJ 196-2010 §3.4.8/§4.0.9, GB 5144-2006
  §10.2, GB 55034-2022 §3.4.7). The measurement **height/location is specified**
  (最大高度处 / 最高处), the averaging time is **not**.
- The only place an averaging window is printed is **GB 6067.3—202X (DRAFT) §10.3.3**:
  anemometers shall measure the **mean gust speed within a 3 s interval (3 s 时距内的平均阵风
  速度)**, and **GB 6067.1-2010 §9.6.1.2** requires the alarm device to display
  **瞬时风速 (instantaneous wind speed)**.
- JGJ 33-2012's 条文说明 to §4.1.14 defines 风速 as "施工现场风速，包括地面和高耸设备高处
  风速" — i.e. the 12.0 m/s normal-lifting figure is **not** height-qualified, while the
   erection figures (9.0 / 12 / 13 m/s) **are** qualified to the machine's maximum height.

*End of file.*
